## What's new in 0.2.0

- **Send a week to Garmin from anywhere.** The calendar has a button for the week on screen and for each week after it, and all three Today looks (One Big Day, Lane Lines, Coach Chat) can send the week without leaving the screen.
- **See your week the way Garmin has it.** The Garmin page now shows the next 7 days from your Garmin Connect calendar, and you can delete some or all of the scheduled sessions there.
- **The Garmin page follows your look.** It now matches the home screen look you picked in setup.
- **Help site.** A step-by-step guide to installing and using flexTri is at https://omeravni78.github.io/flextri/

To update, install this version over the old one. Your plan and check-ins are kept.
