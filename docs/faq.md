# Questions

### Where is my data?

On your computer only. Your plan, check-ins and the Garmin login are kept in your user folder:

- Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\flexTri`
- Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/flexTri`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/flextri`

There is no flexTri account and no flexTri server. Each install holds one athlete.

### How do I update flexTri?

Download the new version from the [download page](https://github.com/omeravni78/flextri/releases/latest) and install it over the old one, the same way you installed it the first time. Your plan and check-ins stay.

### How do I stop it?

Press **Quit** in the top right of the app, or use the flexTri icon in the taskbar or menu bar. If you start flexTri while it is already running, it just opens the existing one in your browser.

### Can I use it on my phone?

flexTri runs on your computer. The screens are built for phones too, so you can use it in a narrow browser window, and every look works on a small screen.

### I missed a whole week. What now?

Use **Edit my setup** and set **Start training on** to today. flexTri fits the plan again from today to your race date. Your logged sessions stay.

### My race date moved.

Change it in **Edit my setup**. You'll see what changes before you save.

### Why did a session change?

Open the day in the [calendar](calendar.html): every change is noted on the session, with the reason (for example "eased after your check-in on Wed 14 Oct"). The [rules](today.html#the-rules-behind-it) are listed on the Today page.

### Which plans are there?

The setup lists them, with how many weeks each was written for. The first real plan is the free [8-week Olympic plan by Marilyn Chychota](https://www.triathlete.com/training/8-week-triathlon-training-plan-olympic-distance/) from Triathlete.com, with swims in meters.

### Windows or macOS won't let me open it.

That's expected for now. See the [install steps](install.html) for the one-time "Run anyway" or "Open Anyway".
