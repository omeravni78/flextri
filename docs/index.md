# Your triathlon plan, fitted to your life

<p class="lead">flexTri takes a training plan written for a fixed number of weeks, fits it to the weeks you actually have until race day, and adjusts the days ahead every time you tell it how a session went.</p>

<a class="btn" href="https://github.com/omeravni78/flextri/releases/latest">Download flexTri</a>

## How it works

1. **You pick a plan and your race date.** An 8-week plan with 10 weeks to go gets stretched; with 6 weeks to go it gets shortened. The taper before race day always stays as the coach wrote it.
2. **You pick your days.** Which days you can train, which day is the long ride, the long run, the brick, and your swim days. flexTri spreads the rest of each week around them.
3. **Every day, one tap.** Open the app, see today's session, and tell flexTri how it went: *Done, felt good*, *Done, felt hard*, *Cut it short* or *Skipped it*. It shows you exactly what that changed in the coming days.
4. **Your watch gets the workouts.** If you use Garmin, send the week to Garmin Connect and sync your watch.
5. **Race day.** When the plan is done, flexTri shows a race summary: sessions done, key sessions, your longest streak and hours per sport.
